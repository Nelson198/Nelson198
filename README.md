## 👋 About me

I'm a **Senior Data Software Engineer** in Portugal 🇵🇹, building data pipelines and platforms that hold up at scale. A few things I've worked on:

### 🛡️ Cybersecurity ratings at terabyte scale
At **Bitsight**, I build pipelines that process terabytes of data into the security ratings customers rely on to understand their footprint and fix vulnerabilities. Every run is tied to an SLA, so reliability isn't optional.

### 🚗 Analytics for BMW vehicles and sales
At **Critical TechWorks**, I designed fact and dimension models for BMW vehicle data and automotive market sales, processed incrementally with **AWS Glue** and **Apache Iceberg**.

### 🎓 Research on data lake updates
My MSc thesis in Computer Software Engineering at the **University of Minho** focused on **HyLake**, a fine-grained update mechanism for data lakes.

I enjoy working with big data and distributed systems: building pipelines that process large volumes of data, and the backend services that make that data useful in real products. I like complex problems, because solving them is how I keep learning and growing as an engineer.
